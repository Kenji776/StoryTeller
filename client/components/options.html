    Combat Style
    Tactical battle map Fights happen on a grid you can see and move on, instead of in description alone

    On: every creature stands somewhere. A map opens when a fight starts, your reachable squares are highlighted on your turn, and you click where to go before you act. Cover, line of sight and reach are measured by the server — a wall really blocks a spell, and you cannot strike something thirty feet away.
    Off: combat is narrated. Faster, looser, and the Dungeon Master is free to improvise, but "I take cover" or "I stay out of reach" is flavour rather than a rule.
    Safe to change between fights. Switching it off mid-fight ends the current map.
